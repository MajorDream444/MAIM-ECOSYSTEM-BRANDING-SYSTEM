01 The Master Asset Doctrine

Stop making content. Start mining the room.

Every week Major teaches one live room. That single hour is the most valuable raw material the movement produces. Everything else — clips, articles, prompts, lessons, merch — is refined from it. We never start from a blank page; we start from the recording.

PRINCIPLE 01
One Live Room

Marketing has exactly one job — get people into the weekly live. Everything downstream depends on a full room.

PRINCIPLE 02
Capture Everything

Record, transcribe, and tag the moment it ends. An un-captured live is value that evaporates.

PRINCIPLE 03
Multiply Relentlessly

One hour becomes a week of clips, an article, a prompt pack, a lesson, and merch signals. Refine, don't recreate.

PRINCIPLE 04
Archive Forever

Every refined asset lands in the Vault — named, searchable, and re-usable. The library compounds week over week.

THE STRATEGIC INSIGHT

Promotion drives one action: fill the live room. After the live, Hanzo, Google Meet, ChatGPT and Claude turn the recording and transcript into the week's content, curriculum, community prompts, and Vault assets.

THE DOCTRINE

Don't scale your effort.
Scale your hour.

09 Merch + Artifact Signal Layer

The room tells us what to make.

The Vault doesn't sell — it signals. When a line lands, a phrase repeats, or the chat lights up, that's a flag for the Merch & Artifacts team (Vol 09 parent) to consider a drop.

NO CHECKOUT · NO TOKEN GATING · SIGNALS ONLY

SIGNAL · QUOTABLE
Phrase → Apparel

A quotable that keeps resurfacing is a candidate for a tee, hoodie, or sticker in the culture lane.

SIGNAL · MILESTONE
Moment → Artifact

Anniversary lives, milestone rooms, and guest specials flag a limited crown-coin artifact.

SIGNAL · POWERED-BY
Demand → Hanzo

Production routes through the Hanzo Powered-By assets (Vol 08). The Vault hands off the signal — not the storefront.
