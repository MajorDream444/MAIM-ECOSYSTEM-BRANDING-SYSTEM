15 Do & Don't

Rules of the road.

✓ DO
Lead with black + white; let gold do the heavy lifting.
Use the tri-bar (green·gold·blue) under headlines and CTAs.
Keep the crown and "MD" mark on solid backgrounds.
Put a real human in the frame — humans are the hero.
Reserve orange for the single most important action.
✕ DON'T
Don't stretch, recolor, or re-typeset the "MD" monogram.
Don't use all five accents at once — pick one per layout.
Don't place gold text on white or green on blue.
Don't add gradients, drop shadows or glow to flat type.
Don't crowd the layout — protect the clear space.
