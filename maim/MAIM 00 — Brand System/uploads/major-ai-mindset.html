What is the Major AI Mindset

AI is a skill.
Confidence
is the foundation.

The Major AI Mindset is a framework for people who want to use AI with clarity — not panic. It is not a technical course. It is an alphabet of ideas, habits, and principles that help you build confidence, take action, and grow over time.

Whether you are just starting or already building systems, the ABCs meet you where you are. Each letter is a lesson. Each lesson is a move you can make today.

Three Levels

Meet yourself where you are.

The same 26 letters teach different things depending on where you are in your journey. Each level shares the spine — the concepts deepen as you grow.

Level 01
Beginner
Build Confidence

You do not need to know how AI works. You need to know how to start. The Beginner level teaches simple use, everyday confidence, and the habit of showing up. One letter, one action, one win at a time.

  • → Ask better questions
  • → Try one tool, one task
  • → Build belief through action
Level 02
Builder
Design Workflow

You are already using AI. Now you want it to work for you — not just when you ask, but as part of how you operate. The Builder level is about designing repeatable flows and turning habits into systems.

  • → Create repeatable workflows
  • → Stack tools intentionally
  • → Document your process

Coming next · Medium level

Level 03
Architect
Build Systems

You are building for scale. The Architect level teaches systems design, governance, delegation to AI agents, and how to think about AI as infrastructure — not just a tool you use but a layer your operation runs on.

  • → AI-first operating systems
  • → Governance and judgment layers
  • → Multi-agent orchestration
