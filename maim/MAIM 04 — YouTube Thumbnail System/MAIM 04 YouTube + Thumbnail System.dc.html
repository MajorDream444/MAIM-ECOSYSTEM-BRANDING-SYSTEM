07 Title / Thumbnail A–B Test Board

Test before you trust.

Run two cuts of every marquee video. Same lesson, different promise. Pick the winner on the 2-second test, then ship.

ACURIOSITY CUT
Most people don't need more AI tools.

DECISION RULE

Show both to 3 people on a phone for 2 seconds each. Whoever can repeat the promise back — that's the cut. Log the winner in the file name with _win.
