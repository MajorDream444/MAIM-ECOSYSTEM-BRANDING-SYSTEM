Workflow Library

Ten sprints, covering the full client lifecycle end to end — mirroring the Template Library's structure so every operational step and every document it produces stay in sync.

Every workflow answers four questions, in order: what starts it, what happens, what gets produced, what happens next.

Outputs

Sprint 01 — Lead to Discovery

Nothing in this sprint produces a client-facing document — no TEMP-XXX template is referenced. It ends with a completed meeting and internal notes that seed Sprint 02.

Discovery Meeting
A scheduled, confirmed conversation with the qualified lead — the event this sprint exists to produce.
Discovery Notes
Internal record of what was learned — feeds directly into TEMP-006 Discovery Report in Sprint 02.
Qualified Opportunity
The lead is confirmed as a real, budgeted opportunity — the gate that lets Sprint 02 begin.

WF-003
Outputs

Sprint 03 — Proposal to Agreement

This sprint's job is converting an offer into a binding commitment — its three outputs are three states of sign-off, not three new documents.

Approved Proposal
TEMP-001 with its Approval Chain cleared — the trigger that authorizes drafting the SOW.
Signed SOW
TEMP-002 with the Client Sponsor's row in its Approval Block moved from Pending to Approved.
Signed Agreement
The completed Signature Block on TEMP-002 — the gate that lets Sprint 04 (Agreement to Payment) begin.
