Sprint 01 — Discovery Playbook

Purpose

Standardize every discovery session so its quality never depends on who happens to be running it.

Flow
Preparation
↓
Research
↓
Discovery Meeting
↓
Capture Notes
↓
Business Diagnosis
↓
Next Steps
This playbook governs execution of WF-001's Discovery Meeting steps — it doesn't change what WF-001 defines, only how a person or agent carries it out.
Decision Points
After Research
If public information contradicts what the lead stated, flag it for the meeting rather than assuming an error.
After Diagnosis
If the business problem doesn't match a package in the Client Package Library, escalate to PKG-010 Custom Engagement before proposing.
PB-001
Step-by-Step Process

Sprint 01 — Discovery Playbook

01
Confirm the meeting, send the calendar invite, and share a one-line agenda 24 hours ahead.
02
Research the prospect's public presence — site, recent news, any shared materials — for 15 minutes minimum.
03
Run the meeting using the standard discovery question set; let the client talk more than you do.
04
Capture notes live and read back the three biggest points before the call ends.
05
Within 24 hours, convert raw notes into a structured Business Diagnosis (TEMP-006 Discovery Report).
06
Send the client a short Next Steps note confirming what happens before they hear from MAIM again.
Quality Checklist
☐ Agenda sent at least 24 hours before the meeting
☐ Notes captured and read back to the client before the call ends
☐ Discovery Report completed within 24 hours of the meeting
☐ Next Steps sent before the client has to ask what's next
AI Assistance Opportunities

An AI agent can draft the pre-meeting research brief from public sources, transcribe and structure live notes into the Discovery Report format, and draft the Next Steps note for human review before sending.

Sprint 02 — Proposal Playbook

Purpose

Create consistent proposals regardless of who drafts them or how complex the engagement is.

Flow
Discovery Notes
↓
Pricing
↓
Proposal Draft (TEMP-001)
↓
Internal Review
↓
Client Proposal
↓
Follow-up
This playbook governs execution of WF-002 — it doesn't change what WF-002 defines, only how the Proposal gets built.
Decision Points
At Pricing
If the scope matches no existing Package, route to PKG-010 Custom Engagement before drafting pricing.
At Internal Review
Any Proposal above a set value threshold requires a second reviewer before it reaches the client.
PB-002
Step-by-Step Process

Sprint 02 — Proposal Playbook

01
Pull the Discovery Report and confirm the diagnosed problem matches an existing Client Package.
02
Build pricing using the matched Package's Pricing Table as the starting structure.
03
Draft the Proposal in TEMP-001, writing the Executive Summary last so it reflects the finished document.
04
Route to Internal Review; a second reviewer checks pricing math and scope-to-diagnosis fit.
05
Send the Client Proposal with a specific date for the walkthrough call, not an open-ended "let me know."
06
If no response within 3 business days after the walkthrough, send one Follow-up before escalating internally.
Review Checklist
☐ Pricing matches an approved Package or has a documented custom rationale
☐ Executive Summary written after the rest of the Proposal, not before
☐ Second reviewer has signed off for any above-threshold Proposal
☐ Walkthrough call scheduled at time of sending, not left open-ended
AI Assistance Opportunities

An AI agent can draft the Proposal body from the Discovery Report and matched Package, flag pricing that deviates from Package norms for reviewer attention, and draft the Follow-up message on schedule for human approval before sending.

Flow
Weekly Checklist
↓

↓
Reports (TEMP-007)
↓

↓

Governs execution of WF-007's recurring loop — the single most-repeated playbook in the library, run once per project per week for the life of every active engagement.
Decision Points
At Risk Review
Any risk rated red escalates to the Engagement Lead same-day, not at the next scheduled meeting.
PB-004
Step-by-Step Process

Sprint 04 — Weekly Operations Playbook

01
Run the Weekly Checklist first thing Monday: task status, blockers, upcoming deadlines.
02
Hold internal team sync before any client-facing meeting that week.
03
Compile the Weekly Report (TEMP-007) from checklist and meeting outcomes.
04
Review risks against the project's risk log; rate each red, yellow, or green.
05
Send the Client Update with the Weekly Report attached, before end of day Friday.
Quality Review Checklist
☐ Weekly Checklist completed before any client meeting that week
☐ Every open risk has a current rating, not a carried-over one
☐ Red risks escalated same-day, not held for the report
☐ Client Update sent by end of day Friday, every week
AI Assistance Opportunities

An AI agent can compile the Weekly Report draft from task-tracker and meeting data, flag risks whose rating hasn't changed in three or more cycles for human re-review, and draft the Client Update for approval before sending.

Sprint 05 — Approval Playbook

Purpose

Ensure approvals are consistent — the same bar for "done," milestone to milestone and reviewer to reviewer.

Flow
Milestone Complete
↓
Review Process
↓
Approval Checklist (COMP-501)
↓
Acceptance Recorded
Governs execution of WF-008 — the gate every milestone must clear the same way, whether the reviewer is a person or an AI agent doing a first pass.
Decision Points
At Review
If Acceptance Criteria are not fully met, the milestone returns to Weekly Operations (PB-004) rather than being approved with caveats.
PB-005
Step-by-Step Process

Sprint 05 — Approval Playbook

01
Confirm the milestone's deliverables match what was scoped in the Project Plan — no silent scope changes.
02
Run Internal Review against the Acceptance Criteria before the client ever sees the milestone.
03
Present the milestone to the client with the Approval Checklist visible, not hidden in a separate document.
04
Record Approval in COMP-501 the same day it's given — never retroactively.
Approval Checklist
☐ Deliverables match scoped Milestone definition exactly
☐ Internal Review completed before client presentation
☐ Acceptance Criteria shown to client alongside the deliverable
☐ Approval recorded same-day, not after the fact
AI Assistance Opportunities

An AI agent can run a first-pass check of deliverables against the scoped Acceptance Criteria and flag discrepancies before Internal Review, reducing what a human reviewer needs to check manually.

Decision Points
At Outstanding Balance
Any invoice unpaid past 15 days triggers a direct call from the Engagement Lead, not another automated reminder.
PB-006
Step-by-Step Process

Sprint 06 — Financial Operations Playbook

01
Generate the Invoice (TEMP-003) within 24 hours of the Agreement being signed.
02
Log the invoice in the payment tracker with its due date and terms.
03
Send an automated reminder at 3 days before due date, and again on the due date if unpaid.
04
On payment, issue the Receipt (TEMP-004) same-day and update the tracker.
05
If unpaid past 15 days, the Engagement Lead calls the client directly before any further automated contact.
Quality Checklist
☐ Invoice sent within 24 hours of signed Agreement
☐ Payment tracker updated same-day as any status change
☐ Receipt issued same-day as payment received
☐ 15-day overdue invoices get a human call, not another email
AI Assistance Opportunities

An AI agent can generate the Invoice from the signed Agreement's terms, send scheduled reminders automatically, and flag any invoice crossing the 15-day threshold for the Engagement Lead to call.

Sprint 08 — Support Playbook

Purpose

Manage post-delivery support so a client's confidence doesn't drop the moment the formal engagement ends.

Flow
Support Intake
↓
Prioritization
↓
Resolution
↓
Follow-up
↓
Client Satisfaction
Governs execution of WF-010's Support step — the playbook that keeps the relationship warm between formal engagements.
Decision Points
At Prioritization
Anything affecting the client's ability to operate the delivered system is urgent; everything else is routine.
PB-008
Step-by-Step Process

Sprint 08 — Support Playbook

01
Log the request through the portal at Support Intake, capturing what, when, and impact.
02
Prioritize as Urgent or Routine within the stated service level from the Support Agreement.
03
Resolve and document the fix or answer in plain language the client can reference later.
04
Follow up within 48 hours to confirm the resolution actually solved the problem.
05
Log a brief Client Satisfaction note — this feeds Sprint 10's expansion and referral conversations.
Quality Checklist
☐ Request acknowledged within the stated service level
☐ Urgent requests never sit in the Routine queue
☐ Resolution documented in plain language, not internal jargon
☐ Follow-up completed within 48 hours of resolution
AI Assistance Opportunities

An AI agent can triage incoming requests against the Urgent/Routine rule, draft the plain-language resolution note, and schedule the 48-hour follow-up automatically for human confirmation.

Sprint 10 — Internal Operations Playbook

Purpose

Run the MAIM service business itself — the playbook that keeps every other playbook honest.

Flow
Team Responsibilities
↓
Weekly Reviews
↓
KPI Review
↓
Continuous Improvement
↓
Knowledge Capture
The only playbook that governs no single Workflow — it governs all of them, closing the loop back into every Continuous Improvement Notes field in PB-001 through PB-009.
Decision Points
At KPI Review
Any KPI missed two cycles running triggers a formal playbook revision, not just a verbal note to try harder.
PB-010
Step-by-Step Process

Sprint 10 — Internal Operations Playbook

01
Confirm every active engagement has a named owner for each playbook role — no ambiguous ownership.
02
Run a weekly internal review of all active engagements against their Weekly Operations Playbook (PB-004) checklists.
03
Review KPIs — proposal conversion, on-time delivery, support response times — on a fixed monthly cadence.
04
Where a KPI or playbook Continuous Improvement Note points to a recurring gap, revise the playbook, not just the individual instance.
05
Capture the revision and its rationale so the next person understands why the process changed.
Quality Checklist
☐ Every active engagement has a named owner per role
☐ Weekly review completed against every active engagement, not a sample
☐ KPIs reviewed on a fixed monthly cadence, never skipped
☐ Every playbook revision has a captured rationale
AI Assistance Opportunities

An AI agent can aggregate KPI data across all active engagements, flag playbooks whose Continuous Improvement Notes repeat the same issue across cycles, and draft the revision rationale for human sign-off.
