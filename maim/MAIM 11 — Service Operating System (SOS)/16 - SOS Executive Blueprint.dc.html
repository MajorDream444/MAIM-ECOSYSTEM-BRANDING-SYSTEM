EXEC-001
Mission & Why It Exists

Sprint 01 — Executive Vision

Mission Statement

Enable every future client engagement to be delivered through one consistent, reusable, AI-assisted operating model — the same Success Statement carried through Page 15's Implementation Roadmap.

Why SOS Exists

Most agencies stop at branding. Most consultants stop at playbooks. SOS goes further — it is the operating architecture underneath the business itself: a shared visual language, a defined document system, and a governed AI workforce, all built to run together rather than assembled ad hoc per engagement.

Key Benefits
· One consistent client experience across every engagement type
· No document created outside the approved system
· Full visibility into status without asking anyone directly
· AI agents draft and validate; humans stay in control of every client-facing decision

EXEC-002
Problem & Opportunity

Sprint 02 — The SOS Story

The Problem

Every one-off document is a decision made in isolation — its own layout, its own tone, its own metadata (or none). Multiplied across proposals, invoices, reports, and agreements, that isolation compounds into inconsistency the client eventually notices, and a growing pile of small decisions nobody remembers making.

The Opportunity

Design the reusable pieces once — Components, Templates, Workflows — and every future document assembles from approved parts instead of being drawn from scratch. The same discipline that makes a brand system work for visuals works for operations.

EXEC-002
Evolution, Philosophy & Required Output

Sprint 02 — The SOS Story

The Evolution
Foundation and Components first, then Templates and Workflows, then Playbooks and Skills, then Automations and governance — each layer built only once the one beneath it was solid (mirroring IMP-001's guiding principle).
Why Systems Matter
A system outlives any single person's memory of "how we usually do it" — new team members, contractors, and AI agents can all operate consistently from the same defined source.
Lessons Learned
Naming conventions and metadata standards (Page 10) had to come before automation (Page 09) — trying to automate an inconsistent system just automates the inconsistency faster.
Design Philosophy
Restraint over decoration, hierarchy by type not color, every document traceable — the same principles from Page 01's Foundation Board, applied at the scale of an entire operating system.
Future Vision
A system mature enough that a new service line can be added by assembling existing Components and Skills, rarely by inventing something new.
Completion Criteria
The Journey and Story read clearly to someone who has never seen a MAIM client artifact, without referencing any specific client by name.

Definitions

Sprint 05 — Object Model

Component
A reusable design element with defined layout, type, and spacing rules.
Template
A fixed document structure assembled from Components.
Workflow
A defined internal sequence with a trigger, steps, and an output.
Artifact
Any generated document or record with standard SOS metadata.
Project
A single active or completed client engagement.
Client
A company or individual with an active or historical MAIM engagement.

EXEC-008
Governance & Versioning

Sprint 08 — Governance & Versioning

Governance Model
Domain Maintainers (GH-001) own their own object types; cross-cutting changes require the Operations Lead's sign-off regardless of domain.
Versioning Strategy
Semantic versioning everywhere — a MAJOR bump is a breaking change, MINOR is backward-compatible addition, PATCH is a correction (GH-009).
Approval Process
All changes move through a pull request; no direct commits to the canonical source, even by Maintainers.
Release Cycle
Individual objects version independently until formal release/* branches activate — a reserved capability, not yet in use.
EXEC-008
Improvement & Required Output

Sprint 08 — Governance & Versioning

Continuous Improvement
Real engagement lessons feed a single cycle — Observation → Lesson Captured → Pattern Identified → Change Proposed → Decision Approved → System Updated (NT-009).
Change Management
Every change is logged with what changed, why, objects affected, and migration requirements — never a silent edit (GH-009).
