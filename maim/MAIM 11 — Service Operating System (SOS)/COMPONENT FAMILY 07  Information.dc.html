Component Family 07

Information

Six flavors of one shape — a left-bordered box that interrupts the reading flow to flag something the reader shouldn't skim past.

Only the border color, eyebrow word, and icon glyph change between variants — the box geometry never does.

COMP-601
Primary Variant

Callout

Purpose: Neutral emphasis — a fact worth pausing on, no urgency implied. Used on: any document body, to lift a key figure or statement out of running text.

CalloutEngagements under this SOW renew automatically each quarter unless cancelled with 30 days' notice.
Layout
3px left border, no fill, no icon — the plainest of the six, reserved for neutral emphasis.
Typography
Eyebrow in tracked mono 9.5px; body in Archivo 400, 12px.
Spacing
12/16px padding, max-width 560px so the box never spans the full text column.
Accessibility
Eyebrow word is mandatory even at neutral emphasis — a border alone never carries meaning.
Print / PDF
Border renders at full 3px in print; kept whole, never split by a page break.
COMP-602
Primary Variant

Warning

Purpose: Flags a real risk or consequence the reader must not miss. Used on: Agreements (liability, termination clauses) and Onboarding Guides (irreversible actions).

WarningMissed payments beyond 15 days past due suspend all active service delivery until the balance is cleared.
Layout
Orange border with a 6% orange tint fill — the only Information variant permitted a background tint.
Typography
Eyebrow "Warning" always in orange, uppercase, tracked — matches the system's single-orange-accent rule.
Spacing
Identical box geometry to Callout: 12/16px padding, 560px max-width.
Accessibility
The word "Warning" is never abbreviated or replaced with an icon alone.
Print / PDF
Tint remains visible at low-ink/grayscale print settings — verified against 15% minimum fill contrast.
COMP-603
Primary Variant

Tip

Purpose: Optional, helpful, low-stakes — the reader loses nothing by skipping it. Used on: Onboarding Guides and Weekly Reports.

TipExport your weekly report as a PDF before sharing externally — it keeps every component's spacing intact.
Layout
Blue border, no fill — the informational-blue accent reserved for this and Client Review states only.
Typography
Same type scale as Callout; eyebrow color follows the blue accent.
Spacing
Identical geometry to Callout and Warning — the family never varies box shape by state.
Accessibility
Framed as genuinely optional in copy — never phrased in a way that implies a requirement.
Print / PDF
Safe to omit from condensed print layouts if space is tight — the only Information variant marked optional-to-print.
COMP-604
Primary Variant

Best Practice

Purpose: Recommends a proven approach based on prior engagements. Used on: Onboarding Guides and Executive Summaries.

Best PracticeSchedule your weekly sync for Monday morning — teams that do report 20% faster resolution on open items.
Layout
Green border, no fill — the culture-green accent used here for "proven, recommended" rather than status.
Typography
"Best Practice" is the longest eyebrow label in the family — tracking reduces slightly (0.1em) to keep it on one line.
Spacing
Same 12/16px padding, 560px max-width as all Information variants.
Accessibility
Always cites the basis for the recommendation (a stat, a pattern) — never asserted without support.
Print / PDF
Green holds distinct from the Approved/Completed green states elsewhere since it never appears in the same component (badges vs. info boxes don't compete for meaning).
COMP-605
Primary Variant

AI Recommendation

Purpose: Attributes a suggestion specifically to MAIM's AI systems, distinct from a human best practice. Used on: Weekly Reports and Executive Summaries generated with AI-assisted analysis.

AI RecommendationBased on this week's ticket volume, consider reallocating one support hour from Tuesday to Thursday.
Layout
Gold border with a 6% gold tint — the only Information variant sharing the tint treatment with Warning, distinguishing it as system-generated, not human-authored.
Typography
Eyebrow always reads "AI Recommendation" in full — never shortened to "AI Rec" or an icon.
Spacing
Same geometry as the family; the gold tint is the sole differentiator from Callout.
Accessibility
Always disclosed as AI-generated in the label itself — never presented as if written by a human analyst.
Print / PDF
Same tint-contrast rule as Warning — legible at low-ink print settings.
COMP-606
Primary Variant

Important Note

Purpose: The strongest non-Warning emphasis — must-read, but not a risk or consequence. Used on: any document, ahead of a step that's easy to miss but not dangerous to skip.

ImportantThis proposal's pricing is valid for 30 days from the Effective Date shown in the metadata block above.
Layout
The one variant with a thicker (4px) border and full white-clarity color — visually the loudest without using orange.
Typography
Eyebrow "Important" in full ivory, not gold or a culture color — reserves those hues for their specific meanings.
Spacing
Same 12/16px padding; the thicker border is the only spacing deviation in the family.
Accessibility
Reserved for genuinely time- or condition-bound facts — overuse dilutes its one distinguishing signal (border weight).
Print / PDF
4px border holds clearly at any print resolution, including black-and-white fax-quality output.
Combination Example

Information Components, Assembled

An excerpt from an Onboarding Guide, showing how Information components punctuate body copy without ever competing with each other on the same page:

TipBookmark your client portal login — you'll use it every week.
WarningRevoking portal access for a team member cannot be undone from your side — contact support instead.
Best PracticeReview your Weekly Report every Friday before it rolls into the next cycle.

Each box (601–606) shares identical geometry — only border color, eyebrow word, and fill/no-fill change. No two variants appear back-to-back with the same border color, so the eye always registers a state change.
