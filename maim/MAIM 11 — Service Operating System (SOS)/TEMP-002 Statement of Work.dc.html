01 · Project Overview
Project Overview

This Statement of Work formalizes the phased advisory engagement described in Proposal PROP-2026-00512: a 16-week program combining strategic advisory, implementation support, and standardized quarterly reporting for Aurelia Capital Partners.

Objectives

Consolidate reporting across three regional teams, reduce leadership decision cycles from 9 days to 3, and cut new-hire ramp time from 6 weeks to 3.

02 · Scope
Scope

Stakeholder discovery interviews (up to 6), a current-state operational audit, a strategic roadmap, and rollout of a unified quarterly reporting template across all three regions.

Deliverables

Discovery findings report, operational audit and roadmap document, and a standardized quarterly reporting template with training for regional leads.

Assumptions

Client will provide access to regional leads within 5 business days of kickoff and designate one internal point of contact for the duration of the engagement.

This engagement does not include software implementation, staff augmentation, or ongoing managed reporting services beyond the rollout period.
