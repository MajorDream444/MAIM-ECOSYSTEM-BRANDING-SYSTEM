Option 01 · Editorial Split
MAJOR AI MINDSET
BEGINNER · LESSON A / 26
A IS FOR
A
Awareness
THE METAPHOR
[ torch lighting one
corner of a dark room ]

Awareness is noticing where the light needs to point.

THE QUESTION
Where in my day could a little help go a long way?
THE LESSON

AI doesn't start with tools. It starts with noticing. Before you open any app, look at your ordinary day — the message you rewrite five times, the email you keep putting off, the caption you can't find words for. That little friction is the signal. Awareness is simply naming one spot where a bit of help would go a long way.

THE MANTRA
AI begins the moment you notice.
AVOID

Thinking you need to learn everything before you start. You don't — you just need to notice one thing.

THE 5-MINUTE ACTION < 5 MIN
Write down three moments today where you felt stuck, slow, or unsure. Circle the one that costs you the most time. That's your starting point.
REFLECT / DISCUSS

What task have you been avoiding simply because it drains you?

MEMORY HOOK
"You can't automate what you haven't noticed."
