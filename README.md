Design and branding for the **Major AI Mindset (MAIM)** ecosystem, created by Major Dream Williams in Claude Design.

One ecosystem, two brands that share the same system:

| Brand | Mark | Folder | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Major AI Mindset** | MD monogram on the woven crown | [`maim/`](maim/) | built (projects 00 to 11 below) |
